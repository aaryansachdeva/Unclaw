## Design Context

### Users
Desktop users who run an Unreal Engine pixel-streamed 3D AI character as a companion. They interact via text and voice through a narrow always-on-top Electron window (340x620px) that floats on the side of their display. The character responds with animations, speech, and moods. Users range from tech-savvy early adopters to casual users who just want an AI friend on their screen.

### Brand Personality
**Sleek, intimate, alive.** UnClaw is a living companion, not a tool. The interface should feel like a portal to another being — premium, breathing, warm despite the dark palette. Think luxury device that wakes up to greet you.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference**: Apple Vision Pro — glass panels floating in space, spatial computing, refined translucency
- **Theme**: Dark-only. Void-black (#050506) base with teal accent (#2dd4bf). Warm off-white text (#f0ede8).
- **Materials**: Frosted glass (backdrop-blur with white-alpha backgrounds), soft borders, no heavy shadows
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans — warm, slightly rounded, personality-driven
- **Motion**: Framer Motion — subtle, purposeful. Spring-based transitions. Nothing flashy, everything intentional.

### Emotional Goals
- **Presence**: The character feels real, the UI disappears, you forget it's an app
- **Delight**: Small moments of polish — a button that breathes, a transition that flows
- **Confidence**: Rock-solid feel. Nothing broken, nothing janky, everything responds instantly

### Anti-References (Explicitly Avoid)
- ChatGPT / generic chatbot UI (white bubbles, message lists, sterile AI interface)
- Gamer/RGB aesthetic (neon, aggressive angles, gaming overlay vibes)
- Corporate dashboard (cards, charts, sidebar nav, enterprise SaaS)
- Skeuomorphic / heavy (drop shadows, thick borders, fake textures, heavy chrome)

### Design Principles
1. **The stream is the star** — UI is secondary. Minimize chrome. The 3D character should dominate the visual experience.
2. **Glass, not walls** — Use translucent surfaces that feel like they float over the stream, not opaque panels that block it.
3. **Breathe, don't blink** — Animations should feel organic and alive (breathing pulses, smooth springs) not mechanical (blinks, snaps, bounces).
4. **Whisper, don't shout** — Text is small, borders are subtle, accents are sparse. Confidence comes from restraint.
5. **One material** — Everything uses the same frosted glass language: `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` background, `blur(40px) saturate(1.6)`, `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.09)`. Consistency creates premium feel.
